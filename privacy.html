Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what data PogCut ("we") accesses when a content creator ("you") authorizes it, and how that data is used. PogCut is operated by William Gentil, France.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to your channel and to read your channel's name, so we can confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Twitch: your public clips and their download links, through the editor or clip permissions you grant.
  • TikTok and Instagram (only if you enable them): permission to upload videos to your account and to read your account name.
  • Your content: the clips and videos you authorize us to process.

We do not access your viewers' data, your private messages, your analytics, your payment information or any other account data.

How we use it

Only to provide the service you asked for: editing your clips and videos into short videos and publishing them on your own accounts. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted, as secrets of a private GitHub repository, and are only used by the automated publishing job. Videos are processed temporarily and deleted once published. We keep a list of the clips already published (clip identifier, title, date) so the same clip is never published twice.

YouTube API Services

PogCut uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke our access at any time: for Google/YouTube on the Google security settings page, and in the connected-apps settings of Twitch, TikTok and Instagram. Write to us and we will delete your tokens and stored data within 7 days.
