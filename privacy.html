Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what data PogCut ("we") accesses when a content creator ("you") authorizes it, and how that data is used. PogCut is operated by William Gentil, France, who is the data controller.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to your channel (youtube.upload) and to read your channel's name (youtube.readonly), so we can confirm the right channel is connected. We also read public information about your videos (title, most replayed parts, public comments) to find their best moments.
  • Twitch: your clips and their download links, through the clip permissions you grant.
  • TikTok (only if you enable it): your account's display name (user.info.basic) and permission to send videos to your account (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram (only if you enable it): your professional account's name and permission to publish Reels on it.
  • Your content: the clips and videos you authorize us to process.

We do not access your viewers' personal data, your private messages, your analytics, your payment information or any other account data.

How we use it

Only to provide the service you asked for: editing your clips and videos into short videos and publishing them on your own accounts. We do not sell or rent your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Service providers

To run the service, some data goes to these providers, only for the tasks listed:

  • Google Gemini API: the text transcript of a video, to suggest which passages to turn into short videos and their titles.
  • GitHub: hosts the automated job that edits and publishes the videos, and stores the access tokens as encrypted secrets.
  • Discord: the titles and links of the published videos, sent to a private notification channel so we can follow the service.

Transcription and editing run on our own job: your videos are not sent to any other third party.

Google user data

PogCut's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

PogCut uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, security and retention

  • Access tokens: stored encrypted as secrets of a private repository, used only by the publishing job, kept until you revoke access or ask us to delete them.
  • Videos: processed temporarily and deleted once published.
  • Publishing history: clip identifier, title and date of each published video, so the same clip is never published twice; kept while you use the service.
  • Transcripts: kept while you use the service so a video is not transcribed twice; deleted with your other data.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke our access at any time: for Google/YouTube on the Google account permissions page, and in the connected-apps settings of Twitch, TikTok and Instagram (on Instagram and Facebook: Settings, then Apps and websites).

To delete your data, email skb.clips00@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the address you signed up with, or tell us which accounts you connected. We delete your tokens, transcripts and publishing history within 7 days and confirm by email. Videos already published stay on your accounts until you delete them there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its processing, by writing to us. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

The service is meant for creators aged 18 or over, or minors with their legal guardian's consent.

Changes

We will notify connected creators by email before any significant change to this policy.
