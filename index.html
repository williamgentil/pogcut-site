PogCut

Turning creators' best moments into short vertical videos.

PogCut is a service for content creators. With the creator's permission, it finds the best moments of their own live streams and videos, edits them into vertical short videos (subtitles, layout, sound levelling) and publishes them on the creator's own channels: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels.

How it works

  1. The creator authorizes PogCut to access their channels. Access can be revoked at any time.
  2. PogCut selects the most popular clips or the strongest passages of their videos.
  3. Each moment is edited into a vertical short video.
  4. The short is published on the creator's own accounts, or left for their review first.

PogCut only ever works with content belonging to creators who have authorized it, and only publishes on their own accounts.
